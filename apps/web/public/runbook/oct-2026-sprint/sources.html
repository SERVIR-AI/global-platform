Central America: Carbon and biodiversity for national agencies

Costa Rica and Guatemala; El Salvador is ticked in the data matrix without links.

Costa Rica is the strongest region on the host: extent and change for any named area, carbon as area × cited factor, species records, protected areas and the library of reports. Guatemala has its official series, the municipalities to name, factors and species records. Measured: Chomes district since 1996 in 15 s; the Gulf of Nicoya in 12 s; Champerico in 12 s.

Domain pack: Land-cover pack for everything in this table: the stacks (class areas, change, transitions, per-class series), the factor tables multiplied by class areas, the vectors measured inside an area, the feeds and point layers cited, the library. The boundaries are the platform’s shared gazetteer, used by every pack. No mangrove-specific pack exists: mangrove is a class of the land-cover stacks.

Asia: A developer’s ecological-cost check for a site

No Asian rows in the data matrix; one Global Mangrove Watch stack is registered (Demak coast, Central Java).

One coast works by name today (Demak, Central Java, 20 s for 1996 to 2020 through an OpenStreetMap boundary). Everything else is a request away: the global sources load by box in minutes and the species feeds in a second. The developer’s site polygons are the input that turns this into a tool.

Domain pack: Land-cover pack for extent, change and carbon; the risk pack only for the hazard-exposure rows. An app composes the two packs’ receipts; neither pack branches on the use case.
